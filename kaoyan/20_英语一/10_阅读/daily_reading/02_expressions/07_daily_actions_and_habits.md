### `[EXP-001]` bail on (plans / someone)

* **释义与沟通功能**：【动作】临时放鸽子 / 爽约 / 推掉既定安排
* **声音形式**：/beɪld ɒn/（连读流畅平滑，过去式轻带尾音）
* **语体映射**：
  * **正式书面**：cancel an engagement at short notice / fail to honor a prior commitment
  * **中性通用**：back out of the plan / withdraw at the last minute
  * **自然口语**：flake on / ditch / bail on
* **触发情境**：当你想表达“临时推掉聚会”、“鸽了朋友”或“推迟活动”时。
* **句型骨架**：
  * `[sb.] bailed on [plans/dinner] at the last minute because of [reason].`
  * `I had to bail on [sb.] in order to [do sth. urgent].`
* **来源溯源**：[《The Magic of Summer Reading》P5](../01_articles/2026/2026-08-20_the_magic_of_summer_reading/article.md#p5)
* **生命周期**：`L3 · 主动生成` (更新于 2026-08-20)
* **调用与迁移记录**：
  * 2026-08-20：阅读文章捕获并造句（L2）
  * 2026-08-20：在 1 分钟脱稿复述中主动说出 `bailed on family plans`（L3）

### `[EXP-002]` counter the summer slide / learning loss

* **释义与沟通功能**：【动作】遏制 / 逆转长假导致的学力滑坡与知识遗忘
* **声音形式**：/ˈkaʊntə ðə ˈsʌmə slaɪd/（counter 发音清晰有力）
* **语体映射**：
  * **正式书面**：mitigate seasonal learning loss / prevent academic regression
  * **中性通用**：prevent students from falling behind over vacation
  * **自然口语**：stop kids from falling behind over summer break
* **触发情境**：当你想表达“采取措施防止学生/团队能力在假期或停工期退步”时。
* **句型骨架**：
  * `[Measures/Apps] were introduced to counter [the summer slide / learning loss].`
  * `Librarians try to counter the slide by promoting [activities].`
* **来源溯源**：[《The Magic of Summer Reading》P4](../01_articles/2026/2026-08-20_the_magic_of_summer_reading/article.md#p4)
* **生命周期**：`L2 · 提示使用` (更新于 2026-08-20)

### `[EXP-003]` curl up in a cozy nook

* **释义与沟通功能**：【动作】窝在舒适温馨的小角落里（惬意阅读/休息）
* **声音形式**：/kɜːl ʌp ɪn ə ˈkəʊzi nʊk/（curl up 连读 /kɜːl-ʌp/）
* **语体映射**：
  * **正式书面**：sit comfortably in a secluded space
  * **中性通用**：settle in a quiet corner with a book
  * **自然口语**：get super comfy in a quiet corner with a book
* **触发情境**：当你想描绘下雨天、咖啡馆或家中静心阅读的极度舒适场景时。
* **句型骨架**：
  * `She loves to curl up in a cozy nook with a warm cup of tea.`
* **来源溯源**：[《The Magic of Summer Reading》P5](../01_articles/2026/2026-08-20_the_magic_of_summer_reading/article.md#p5)
* **生命周期**：`L2 · 提示使用` (更新于 2026-08-20)

### `[EXP-004]` live up to the hype

* **释义与沟通功能**：【评价/动作】名副其实 / 不负盛名 / 确实如外界宣传得那么好
* **声音形式**：/lɪv ʌp tə ðə haɪp/（live up 连读 /lɪv-ʌp/）
* **语体映射**：
  * **正式书面**：fulfill high public expectations / match external acclaim
  * **中性通用**：be as good as anticipated
  * **自然口语**：it's totally as good as everyone says / it really delivers
* **触发情境**：当某个大火的工具、电影、产品或书籍真正配得上铺天盖地的赞美时。
* **句型骨架**：
  * `The new [product/movie/tech] totally lives up to the hype.`
  * `Whether it will live up to the hype remains an open question.`
* **来源溯源**：[《The Magic of Summer Reading》P7](../01_articles/2026/2026-08-20_the_magic_of_summer_reading/article.md#p7)
* **生命周期**：`L2 · 提示使用` (更新于 2026-08-20)
