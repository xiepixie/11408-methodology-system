# q15_高等数学

## 📋 题目要求 (题面)

已知平面区域

$$
D=\left\{(r,\theta)\mid
2\le r\le2(1+\cos\theta),
-\frac\pi2\le\theta\le\frac\pi2
\right\},
$$

计算

$$
\iint_Dx\,dx\,dy.
$$

---

## 💡 答案与深度解析

**【答案】**

$$
\boxed{5\pi+\frac{32}{3}}.
$$

**【解析】**

在极坐标下

$$
x=r\cos\theta,
\qquad
 dx\,dy=r\,dr\,d\theta.
$$

因此

$$
\begin{aligned}
I
&=\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}
\int_2^{2(1+\cos\theta)}
r^2\cos\theta\,dr\,d\theta\\
&=\frac83\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}
\cos\theta\left[(1+\cos\theta)^3-1\right]d\theta\\
&=\frac83\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}
\left(3\cos^2\theta+3\cos^3\theta+\cos^4\theta\right)d\theta.
\end{aligned}
$$

利用

$$
\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}\cos^2\theta\,d\theta=\frac\pi2,
$$

$$
\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}\cos^3\theta\,d\theta=\frac43,
$$

$$
\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}\cos^4\theta\,d\theta=\frac{3\pi}{8},
$$

得到

$$
\begin{aligned}
I
&=8\cdot\frac\pi2
+8\cdot\frac43
+\frac83\cdot\frac{3\pi}{8}\\
&=4\pi+\frac{32}{3}+\pi\\
&=\boxed{5\pi+\frac{32}{3}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
