# q07_线性代数

## 📋 题目要求 (题面)

设向量组 $\alpha _{1}$ , $\alpha _{2}$ , $\alpha _{3}$ 线性无关，则下列向量组线性相关的是

- **A.** $\alpha _{1}-\alpha _{2}$ , $\alpha _{2}-\alpha _{3}$ , $\alpha _{3}-\alpha _{1}$ ．
- **B.** $\alpha _{1}+\alpha _{2}$ , $\alpha _{2}+\alpha _{3}$ , $\alpha _{3}+\alpha _{1}$ ．
- **C.** $\alpha _{1}-2\alpha _{2}$ , $\alpha _{2}-2\alpha _{3}$ , $\alpha _{3}-2\alpha _{1}$ ．
- **D.** $\alpha _{1}+2\alpha _{2}$ , $\alpha _{2}+2\alpha _{3}$ , $\alpha _{3}+2\alpha _{1}$ ．

---

## 💡 答案与深度解析

**【正确答案】**：`A`

**正确答案：A**

**【解析】**

应选 (A). 因为 $(\alpha _{1}-\alpha _{2})+(\alpha _{2}-\alpha _{3})+(\alpha _{3}-\alpha _{1})=0$ , 所以 $\alpha _{1}-\alpha _{2},\alpha _{2}-\alpha _{3},\alpha _{3}-\alpha _{1}$ 线性相关.

选项 (B) 不对. 因为

$$
(\alpha _{1}+\alpha _{2},\alpha _{2}+\alpha _{3},\alpha _{3}+\alpha _{1})=(\alpha _{1},\alpha _{2},\alpha _{3})\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}=(\alpha _{1},\alpha _{2},\alpha _{3})P_{2},
$$

其中 $|P_{2}|=2\ne 0$ .

选项 (C) 不对, 因为

$$
(\alpha _{1}-2\alpha _{2},\alpha _{2}-2\alpha _{3},\alpha _{3}-2\alpha _{1})=(\alpha _{1},\alpha _{2},\alpha _{3})\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & -2 \\ -2 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}=(\alpha _{1},\alpha _{2},\alpha _{3})P_{3},
$$

其中 $|P_{3}|=-7\ne 0$ .

选项 (D) 不对, 因为

$$
(\alpha _{1}+2\alpha _{2},\alpha _{2}+2\alpha _{3},\alpha _{3}+2\alpha _{1})=(\alpha _{1},\alpha _{2},\alpha _{3})\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}=(\alpha _{1},\alpha _{2},\alpha _{3})P_{4},
$$

其中 $|P_{4}|=9\ne 0$ .
