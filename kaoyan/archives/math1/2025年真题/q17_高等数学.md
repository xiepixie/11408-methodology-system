# q17_高等数学

## 📋 题目要求 (题面)

计算 $\int _{0}^{1}\frac{1}{(x+1)(x^{2}-2x+2)}dx$。

---

## 💡 答案与深度解析

**【答案】** $\frac{3}{10}\ln 2+\frac{1}{10}\pi$

**【解析】**

$$
\begin{pmatrix} \int _{0}^{1}\frac{1}{(x+1)(x^{2}-2x+2)}dx & =\int _{0}^{1}(\frac{A}{x+1}+\frac{Bx+C}{x^{2}-2x+2})dx \\ & =\int _{0}^{1}(x+1\frac{1}{5}+x^{2}-2x+2-\frac{1}{5}x+\frac{3}{5})dx \\ & =\frac{1}{5}\ln |1+x| \mid _{0}^{1} \\ & -\frac{1}{10}\ln |x^{2}-2x+2| \mid _{0}^{1} \\ & +\frac{2}{5}\arctan (x-1) \mid _{0}^{1} \\ & =\frac{3}{10}\ln 2+\frac{1}{10}\pi \end{pmatrix}
$$
