# q02_高等数学

## 📋 题目要求 (题面)

已知级数：① $\sum _{n=1}^{\infty}\sin \frac{n^{3}\pi}{n^{2}+1}$；② $\sum _{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{n}(\frac{1}{\sqrt{3n^{2}}}-\tan \frac{1}{\sqrt{3n^{2}}})$，则

- **A.** ①与②均条件收敛.
- **B.** ①条件收敛，②绝对收敛.
- **C.** ①绝对收敛，②条件收敛.
- **D.** ①与②均绝对收敛.

---

## 💡 答案与深度解析

**【正确答案】**：`B`

**正确答案：B**

【解析】

$\mid \sin \frac{n^{3}\pi}{n^{2}+1} \mid = \mid \sin (\frac{n^{3}\pi}{n^{2}+1}-n\pi ) \mid = \mid \sin \frac{n}{n^{2}+1}\pi \mid \sim \frac{n}{n^{2}+1}\pi \sim \frac{1}{n}\pi$ .

$\sum _{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n}$ 发散 $∴$ 不是绝对收敛.

$\sin \frac{n^{3}\pi}{n^{2}+1}=(-1)^{n}\sin (\frac{n^{3}\pi}{n^{2}+1}-n\pi )=(-1)^{n}\sin \frac{n}{n^{2}+1}\pi$，为交错级数.

$\sin \frac{n}{n^{2}+1}\pi$ 递减， $∴$ 条件收敛.

$\sum _{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{n}(\frac{1}{\sqrt{3n^{2}}}-\tan \frac{1}{\sqrt{3n^{2}}})$ .

$\mid (-1)^{n}(\frac{1}{\sqrt{3n^{2}}}-\tan \frac{1}{\sqrt{3n^{2}}}) \mid = \mid -\frac{1}{3}n^{\frac{2}{3}\times \frac{3}{2}}1+o(\frac{1}{n^{2}}) \mid$ （注：原解析此处 $n$ 的次数书写可能有误，推测应为 $\mid -\frac{1}{3}n^{\frac{4}{3}}1+o(n^{\frac{4}{3}}1) \mid$，按你提供内容提取），实际准确是利用等价无穷小，当 $x\to 0$ 时， $x-\tan x\sim -\frac{1}{3}x^{3}$，令 $x=\frac{1}{\sqrt{3n^{2}}}=n^{-\frac{2}{3}}$，则 $\frac{1}{\sqrt{3n^{2}}}-\tan \frac{1}{\sqrt{3n^{2}}}\sim -\frac{1}{3}n^{-2}$，所以 $\mid (-1)^{n}(\frac{1}{\sqrt{3n^{2}}}-\tan \frac{1}{\sqrt{3n^{2}}}) \mid \sim \frac{1}{3n^{2}}$）

$\sum _{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n^{2}}$ 收敛 $∴\sum _{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{n}(\frac{1}{\sqrt{3n^{2}}}-\tan \frac{1}{\sqrt{3n^{2}}})$ 绝对收敛
